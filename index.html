How we work

From first click to sparkling finish, our process keeps things simple.

01

Select your desired service

Select from one of our 35 different services or packages. View our services page for a full list of services we provide.

02

Book in your desired services

Book your services in for a day and time that is convenient for you. We will ask for some basic information, such as your full name, address, contact number and email.

03

The day of the cleaning

A member of the team will come to your property, fully equipped to complete your desired service. After we have finished, you will receive an invoice via email or SMS.

04

The payment process

After the clean, you can pay using cash, contactless, bank transfer, or over the phone. If you are going to be a regular customer, we can set you up on a direct debit payment plan.
